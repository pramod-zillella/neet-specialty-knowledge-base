### Questions that decide the shortlist

- **Operative ownership:** Get year-wise individual primary-operator MSICS and phaco logs, distinguishing performed, assisted and observed procedures; ask how progression is supervised.
- **Complications and emergency care:** Ask how residents learn supervised complication management and emergency eye care; identify who is available out of hours.
- **Subspecialty breadth:** Verify actual resident postings in retina, cornea, glaucoma, paediatric ophthalmology and oculoplasty, with service load and trainee allocation.
- **Wet lab and equipment:** Inspect a functioning wet lab/simulator, protected practice timetable, microscopes and the resident access policy.

### Questions that decide the shortlist

## Checks for every department

- **Programme and eligibility:** Check exact campus/award, current recognition or NBEMS renewal, intended counselling seats, eligibility and any service/bond/exit liability.
- **Faculty and teaching:** Confirm full-time faculty and who supervises your unit; review recent academic timetables, thesis support and exam outcomes with batch denominators.
- **Costs and stipend:** Obtain each year of tuition, compulsory charges, hostel/mess, deposits, stipend frequency/amount and actual payment dates in writing.
- **Resident welfare:** Speak separately with women residents in different years about rosters, post-call rest, leave, supervision and functioning grievance support; record the date and source.
- **Hostel, language and travel:** Check women's hostel/night transport, actual patient languages and induction support, then verify railhead/passenger-airport travel from the assigned campus and Hyderabad services.
