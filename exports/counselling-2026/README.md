# Counselling guide 2026–27

Official-source snapshot checked 2026-10-25. All times IST. Not a live notification service.

Official-source snapshot for a Telangana-local MBBS graduate comparing MCC, Telangana CQ, Rajasthan open-private and Telangana MQ. All dates/times are IST. Not a live notification service.

## MCC Round 1: joined with Float

- Check that the institute has approved your uploaded documents and your portal shows provisional joining/Float, not merely willingness submitted.
- Fill and lock fresh Round 2 choices within the published window. Put only acceptable replacements above the existing option; an allotment cannot be compared against the old seat afterwards.
- If upgraded, obtain the online relieving letter and complete the new joining process. The old seat is cancelled automatically.
- If not upgraded, the joined earlier seat is retained. Watch later-round resignation rules and mandatory physical reporting after Round 3.

Proper upgrading itself does not forfeit security. Non-joining the upgraded Round 2/3 seat does. College transfer/refund charges remain separate.

## MCC All India counselling

Round 1 result published; reporting 26 October–2 November

AIQ, central/institutional, deemed-university and participating DNB choices share MCC counselling. Each has its own eligibility and college fees.

### What MCC covers

50% AIQ contributed by participating state government colleges; 100% deemed and participating DNB seats; central-university/institute seats with their own internal eligibility. INI-CET is separate. “All MCC seats” is not the same as “50% state-government AIQ”.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 4, 24, 26, 29–41

### Central reservation, not Telangana’s roster

AIQ: SC 15%, ST 7.5%, Central-list OBC-NCL 27%, EWS 10%; PwD 5% horizontal. State BC status alone is not Central OBC-NCL proof. No general women’s quota is listed in this AIQ reservation table. Deemed/minority/NRI and internal seats require their own rules.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 24–27

### Float is online joining in 2026

Submit Float willingness, upload clear mandatory documents and contact the allotted institute’s nodal officer. The institute verifies online. Resolve queries within the reporting window and retain the provisional seat-confirmation record. The bulletin calls for uploads within 2–3 days of final allotment so discrepancies can be resolved; the overall reporting deadline still controls.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 10–12, 54

### Freeze requires physical reporting

Physically report with original documents and admission fees at the allotted institute. A final allotment letter or a Float click alone does not replace completion of joining.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 10–12

### Keep the Round 1 seat while seeking an upgrade

A properly joined Round 1 Float candidate may fill fresh Round 2 choices. If no upgrade occurs, the existing seat is retained. If you decide against Round 2 upgrading, FAQ 19 says not to fill fresh choices; verify the portal status. This retains a seat, not an indefinite right to free resignation.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 54–56

### Upgrades are binding replacements

On a Round 2 upgrade, the old Round 1 seat is automatically cancelled; it cannot be reclaimed. Obtain the online relieving record and complete joining of the new seat. Even a category upgrade at the same college needs fresh admission formalities. Non-joining of the upgraded seat forfeits security.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 44–45, 56, 58

### Switching to a state seat

The Round 1/2 scheme permits participation in state counselling. To take a state allotment, surrender the MCC seat formally: Float resignation online; Freeze resignation physically at the institute. Use the MCC-notified resignation period and save the accepted/generated record. A state allotment does not automatically resign MCC.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 13, 15

### Round 1 free exit has a time limit for joined candidates

A Round 1 non-joiner can free exit. A joined candidate must use the formal resignation process within the period prescribed by MCC. The checked documents do not publish a separate exact resignation cut-off for every cross-counselling switch. Do not substitute the reporting deadline for an unannounced resignation deadline.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 13, 15, 43

### Round 2: forfeiture; further entry needs fresh payment

Non-joining or surrender of a Round 2 allotment forfeits security. The scheme permits eligible candidates who resigned/exited Round 2 to participate in Round 3 with fresh payment. Resignation still requires a permitted window.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 7, 13, 15, 45

### Round 3 and Stray are commitment rounds

Non-joining a Round 3 seat forfeits security and eliminates further MCC participation. Joined Round 3 seats cannot be resigned. Stray allotment must be joined or security is forfeited. Eligibility for Stray also depends on prior allotments/joined status and Centre–State data sharing.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 16, 45–46

### Retained Float seats must physically join after Round 3

After Round 3, physical reporting is mandatory for fresh/upgraded Round 3 allottees and Round 1/2 Float candidates who did not upgrade. Online Float cannot be continued indefinitely.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 12, 16

### AIQ plus deemed: only the higher MCC payment is prescribed

FAQ 9 says candidates opting for both pay only ₹5,000 registration plus ₹2 lakh security. It does not prescribe ₹25,000 plus ₹2 lakh as two independently protected deposits. If both were collected, reconcile receipts with MCC/HLL; do not assume a government allotment limits forfeiture to ₹25,000.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 52–53

### Refunds follow completion of counselling

Eligible security refunds return to the original payment account after counselling, not immediately after each round. Keep that account/card active. Registration fees are non-refundable; tuition refunds and college bond liabilities are separate. The refund FAQ describes initiation within 15 days/working days and completion within 30 days of the relevant MCC notification/list.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 60–62

### 2026 change explicitly identified by MCC

Chapter 3 contrasts the 2025 physical process with the new 2026 Freeze/Float workflow. Use its online-joining and online Float-resignation instructions alongside the round rules; generic physical-reporting text elsewhere in the bulletin must be read in that context.

[MCC PG Information Bulletin and Counselling Scheme 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3e0f7a4d0ef9b84b83b693bbf3feb8e6e/uploads/2026/10/202610082121348550.pdf) — PDF p. 10–13

### MCC admission does not erase a college/state bond

MCC directs candidates to confirm course fees, stipend, service obligations and bond amounts with the institution before choices. The state-government AIQ service policy, central institution, deemed college and DNB hospital can differ. Use the State bonds page for dated evidence, then confirm the actual 2026 contract.

## Telangana Competent Authority Quota

Published registration window ended 23 October; web-option dates pending

For eligible Telangana-local candidates. Covers the state share of government seats and the Competent Authority share of private colleges; the registration notice also includes NIMS Hyderabad.

### Local eligibility must be documented

Local means at least four consecutive years of Telangana study ending in the relevant qualifying examination, subject to the stated exceptions. A Telangana UG-local entrant studying at the specified outside state-wide institution can qualify; a non-local-quota MBBS entrant in Telangana is not automatically local. Other-state candidates are ineligible. Your Telangana MBBS/local claim needs the prescribed study and admission records.

[KNRUHS Competent Authority Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261014110317_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-COMPETENT-AUTHORITY-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 3–4

### Seat pools: government versus private

The state-government share follows exclusion of contributed AIQ seats (normally 50% AIQ / 50% state under the MCC scheme). Private colleges have a 50% Competent Authority share alongside a separate 50% management share. Do not apply a private 50% split to government seats or double count women/service reservations as extra seats. Exact branch counts await the current KNRUHS matrix.

[KNRUHS Competent Authority Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261014110317_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-COMPETENT-AUTHORITY-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 3

### Vertical and horizontal reservations

SC 15% split as SC1 1%, SC2 9%, SC3 5%; ST 10%; BC 29% split A 7%, B 10%, C 1%, D 7%, E 4%. Women 33⅓% and PwD 5% are horizontal. Minority-college seats are for eligible Muslim-minority candidates. The displayed EWS qualifying score is not evidence of a separate 10% EWS seat allocation in this prospectus.

[KNRUHS Competent Authority Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261014110317_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-COMPETENT-AUTHORITY-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 5–6

### In-service seats are a separate horizontal claim

20% in clinical broad specialties and 30% in pre/para-clinical broad specialties are reserved horizontally for eligible in-service candidates under the cited service rules. These are not extra seats, and an ordinary fresh applicant cannot claim them.

[KNRUHS Competent Authority Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261014110317_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-COMPETENT-AUTHORITY-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 6

### Qualification, internship and existing PG study

NEET-PG 2026 qualification, completed internship by 30 September 2026 and permanent Telangana State Medical Council registration are required. The prospectus excludes candidates already studying PG degree/diploma/DNB; get KNRUHS clarification before assuming an existing PG enrolment or cross-counselling switch is eligible. A same-cycle MCC option does not override state conditions.

[KNRUHS Competent Authority Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261014110317_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-COMPETENT-AUTHORITY-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 4–5

### Separate application and web options

Complete CQ registration, payment and certificate uploads; print the application. Provisional/final merit and web-option schedules are published separately. A qualified list or registration is not an allotment. At admission submit originals, application printout and self-attested copies at the allotted college.

[KNRUHS Competent Authority Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261014110317_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-COMPETENT-AUTHORITY-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 7–9, 20

### University fee and tuition are separate

Pay ₹35,000 university fee to download the allotment letter. Tuition is as notified by Telangana and varies by institution/course category. The registration/processing fee is non-refundable. The reviewed prospectus does not establish a separate universally refundable security deposit or the refund treatment of every university-fee situation.

[KNRUHS Competent Authority Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261014110317_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-COMPETENT-AUTHORITY-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 7, 21

### Do not import MCC’s free-exit rules

The CQ prospectus refers to the last date for Free Exit but does not provide that date in the reviewed registration documents. Await the phase-specific KNRUHS notice for reporting, sliding, resignation and exit deadlines. Leaving after the stated free-exit limit attracts the prospectus discontinuation terms.

[KNRUHS Competent Authority Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261014110317_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-COMPETENT-AUTHORITY-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 20–22

### Course discontinuation: ₹50 lakh, with additional conditions

The admission bond states ₹50 lakh on default in completing training and refund of stipend/salary received. Discontinuation after free exit also carries a three-year KNRUHS PG counselling debarment. These are not small registration-deposit penalties; read the signed bond and applicable exit notice.

[KNRUHS Competent Authority Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261014110317_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-COMPETENT-AUTHORITY-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 21–22

### One-year service after completion

Non-service postgraduates must serve the Government of Telangana for one year if required under the government orders. The admission service bond is ₹20 lakh for a PG degree and ₹10 lakh for a diploma. This is separate from the ₹50 lakh course-completion bond.

[KNRUHS Competent Authority Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261014110317_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-COMPETENT-AUTHORITY-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 21

### AP domicile is not an automatic state-quota entitlement

The 2026 prospectus uses G.O.148 dated 28 October 2024 and the local-candidate rules. Do not carry forward an old shared AP/Telangana non-local entitlement. The state-wide-institution exception and non-local UG exclusion must be read exactly. SC subgrouping cites an April 2025 order: neither provision should be advertised as newly introduced in 2026 without the amendment history.

## Rajasthan state counselling

Extended registration ended 24 October; security and choices close 29 October

A Telangana MBBS graduate is eligible in principle for the open 50% share of private seats. These are Rajasthan-administered seats, separate from MCC AIQ.

### Your main route is open private seats

50% of total private-college seats are open to eligible NEET-PG candidates with an NMC-recognised MBBS from anywhere. The other private half and the state-government share have Rajasthan MBBS / specified government-service eligibility through Round 3. Telangana domicile alone does not qualify for those restricted seats.

[Rajasthan NEET-PG Information Booklet 2026-27](https://pg.neetrajasthan.com/notifications/1792302615499.pdf) — PDF p. 15–16

### Government AIQ and Rajasthan private AI are different

50% of government/government-society/RUHS CMS seats go through MCC AIQ; remaining state seats and all private seats go through the Rajasthan Board. A private seat labelled “AI nature” is Rajasthan counselling, not an MCC allotment. Management labels in government-society institutions do not waive state eligibility.

[Rajasthan NEET-PG Information Booklet 2026-27](https://pg.neetrajasthan.com/notifications/1792302615499.pdf) — PDF p. 4, 9–10, 15

### Out-of-state applicants are UR for state-category benefits

Rajasthan’s SC/ST/OBC-NCL/MBC-NCL/EWS benefits are for eligible bonafide Rajasthan candidates. Other-state category candidates are considered UR and must qualify as UR. The booklet states at least 25% women within each category and 5% horizontal PwD; do not assume a Telangana caste certificate or every state reservation benefit transfers to the open-private route.

[Rajasthan NEET-PG Information Booklet 2026-27](https://pg.neetrajasthan.com/notifications/1792302615499.pdf) — PDF p. 17–18

### Internship and council registration

Internship must finish by 30 September 2026. Valid NMC/MCI/state council registration is required; if registered with another state initially, submit Rajasthan Medical Council registration within one month after joining. State-process Post-MBBS DNB/diploma seats are only for regular Rajasthan government in-service candidates, separate from MCC DNB.

[Rajasthan NEET-PG Information Booklet 2026-27](https://pg.neetrajasthan.com/notifications/1792302615499.pdf) — PDF p. 15–16

### Private choices need one ₹5 lakh security payment

Application is ₹4,000 plus charges for your out-of-state route. Private-college choices require ₹5 lakh security in one transaction. The ₹1 lakh government and ₹2 lakh government-society/RUHS management tiers do not need to be added to ₹5 lakh; depositing the higher tier permits lower-tier choices only where otherwise eligible.

[Rajasthan NEET-PG Information Booklet 2026-27](https://pg.neetrajasthan.com/notifications/1792302615499.pdf) — PDF p. 20, 23

### Security is adjustable against tuition after joining

On joining, Rajasthan security is adjusted against tuition. If neither forfeited nor adjusted, it is refunded to the original payment account after counselling. Reconcile the allotment-letter demand and Board payment instructions so adjustment is not confused with a second tuition charge. Registration is non-refundable.

[Rajasthan NEET-PG Information Booklet 2026-27](https://pg.neetrajasthan.com/notifications/1792302615499.pdf) — PDF p. 23, 25

### Report at SMS Jaipur, with one-year tuition

Report in person at the allotted-college desk, Academic Block, SMS Medical College, Jaipur, within the schedule. Pay the complete first-year tuition via NEFT/RTGS to the Board account printed on the allotment letter; bring proof, originals, copies and bonds. Downloading the allotment is not joining. The booklet requires accepted resignation/relieving from another current institution.

[Rajasthan NEET-PG Information Booklet 2026-27](https://pg.neetrajasthan.com/notifications/1792302615499.pdf) — PDF p. 25–29

### Round 1 free exit; retained seats acquire later-round obligations

Non-joining Round 1 does not forfeit security. A joined Round 1 candidate who is not upgraded in Round 2 (or does not participate) may resign without forfeiture within two days of the Round 2 result. After that, the retained seat is treated under Round 2 rules. Formal portal-generated resignation is necessary; offline resignation is not accepted.

[Rajasthan NEET-PG Information Booklet 2026-27](https://pg.neetrajasthan.com/notifications/1792302615499.pdf) — PDF p. 6–7, 11, 23–24

### Round 2 resignation is restricted, not an automatic paid exit

Non-joining a fresh/upgraded Round 2 seat forfeits security and further participation requires re-registration and fresh security. Joined Round 2/retained seats generally cannot be resigned except in specifically notified windows (including identified AIQ switches). Do not assume losing ₹5 lakh alone buys permission to leave a joined course.

[Rajasthan NEET-PG Information Booklet 2026-27](https://pg.neetrajasthan.com/notifications/1792302615499.pdf) — PDF p. 12, 23–24

### Round 3 and Stray restrictions

Round 3 non-joining forfeits security and excludes Stray. Joined/retained Round 3 seats cannot be resigned. Stray non-joining forfeits security and bars further state rounds, with the participation bond terms also relevant. The local-eligibility restriction is relaxed for vacancies after Round 3, but prior allotment/joined-status exclusions remain.

[Rajasthan NEET-PG Information Booklet 2026-27](https://pg.neetrajasthan.com/notifications/1792302615499.pdf) — PDF p. 12, 15, 24

### Fresh choices and irreversible upgrades

Fresh choices are mandatory for Round 2 and Round 3 upgrading. An upgraded seat automatically vacates the earlier seat; originals transfer to the upgraded institution rather than being handed to the candidate. Being content with the old seat after the upgrade does not restore it.

[Rajasthan NEET-PG Information Booklet 2026-27](https://pg.neetrajasthan.com/notifications/1792302615499.pdf) — PDF p. 25–26

### Private course-exit surety is separate from security

A private-college surety can require remaining-course fees if the course is left mid-way. The booklet prohibits advance fees, post-dated cheques or bank guarantees for the remaining two years. Government non-service seats have separate service/surety bonds. A refundable deposit is not the maximum possible liability.

[Rajasthan NEET-PG Information Booklet 2026-27](https://pg.neetrajasthan.com/notifications/1792302615499.pdf) — PDF p. 27–28

### Registration extension overrides only the original registration dates

The live notice published 22 October extended Part 1 to 24 October, 4 PM IST and Part 2 to 24 October, 8 PM IST; a 24 October reminder reiterated completion. No new security/choice dates were found in the checked feed. Keep the original Round 1 schedule for those other events, subject to future amendments.

## Telangana Management Quota

Registration open: deadline 30 October, 4 PM IST

Separate registration from Telangana State/CQ. MQ1, MQ2/NRI and MQ3/institutional seats have distinct eligibility and university fees; the notification also includes Neelima/Anurag University.

### Private management share and the correct denominators

Management is 50% of each private college’s specialty seats: MQ1 is 25%, MQ2/NRI 15%, MQ3/institutional 10% of the TOTAL seats. Within MQ1 only, 85% is Telangana-local reserved and 15% nationally open. As an illustration on 100 total seats, these are 21.25 local MQ1 + 3.75 open MQ1 + 15 MQ2 + 10 MQ3, with actual seats rounded by the official roster/matrix.

[KNRUHS Management Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261022104019_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-MANAGEMENT-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 18–19

### Local reservation is different from caste reservation

MQ has no SC/ST/BC community reservation and no other general reservations listed; reserved-category cut-offs affect exam qualification only. The explicit MQ1 local/open split still applies. Minority-college seats go to eligible Muslim-minority candidates; non-minority conversion is described only for the last phase if eligible minority candidates are unavailable.

[KNRUHS Management Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261022104019_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-MANAGEMENT-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 4, 19–20

### MQ1, MQ2 and MQ3 are not interchangeable

MQ1 includes national applicants subject to the 85/15 local split. MQ2 requires the specified NRI/OCI/NRI-sponsorship proof. MQ3 requires a principal’s sponsorship and the defined institutional connection: own MBBS graduate, employee or employee’s son/daughter. Paying a higher fee alone does not establish MQ2/MQ3 eligibility.

[KNRUHS Management Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261022104019_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-MANAGEMENT-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 4, 18–20

### Medical registration and internship

Recognised MBBS, NEET-PG 2026 qualification, internship completed by 30 September 2026 and permanent Telangana State Medical Council registration are required. Upload documents at application; do not rely on a later opportunity to cure missing eligibility.

[KNRUHS Management Quota prospectus 2026-27](https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/notifications/20261022104019_KNRUHS-MEDICAL-PG-DEGREE-DIPLOMA-ADMISSIONS-UNDER-MANAGEMENT-QUOTA-FOR-THE-AY-2026-27-PROSPECTUS.pdf) — PDF p. 4–7

### ₹15,000 application; university fee on allotment

Registration/processing is ₹15,000 plus transaction charges, non-refundable. Clinical university fees: MQ1 ₹2 lakh; MQ2/MQ3 ₹3 lakh. Non-clinical: MQ1 ₹75,000; MQ2/MQ3 ₹1 lakh. These university fees are paid to download the allotment order, separate from annual tuition, and are not described as MCC-style refundable security deposits.
