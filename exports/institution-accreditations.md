## Which credentials matter?

My shortlisting view: verify course permission first; then weigh the relevant department, case exposure, supervision and resident welfare. Full NABH/JCI are useful hospital-system signals; NABL/CAP are particularly useful laboratory signals; NAAC supplies academic institutional context. None is a stand-alone residency ranking.

| Credential | Value | What it measures | Scope limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMC / statutory course authority | Admission prerequisite | Regulatory permission/recognition and minimum training requirements | Verify permission vs recognition and current intake. INIs have their own statutory/admission framework. |
| NBEMS | Admission prerequisite | Programme accreditation, accredited seat columns and validity month | Not a hospital-wide badge, MD/MS recognition, counselling vacancy or guarantee of training outcomes. |
| NABH full hospital / SHCO | Strong hospital-quality signal | Patient safety, clinical governance, infection control, medication systems and quality processes | Full HCO/SHCO must be distinguished from entry-level certification, empanelment and targeted awards. Does not measure operative autonomy. |
| JCI | Strong international hospital-quality signal | International hospital patient-safety and quality systems | Not required for a good Indian residency. Check the exact campus and current cycle; a chain-wide claim is insufficient. |
| NABL ISO 15189 | Strong laboratory-quality signal | Laboratory quality systems and technical competence for the stated medical-testing scope | Not all hospital laboratories/tests are necessarily covered. Read the scope, standard edition, expiry and suspension/extension status. |
| CAP Laboratory Accreditation Program | Strong international laboratory-quality signal | Laboratory quality and technical practice against CAP programme requirements | Proficiency-testing subscription, membership and a different laboratory in the same chain do not establish LAP accreditation. |
| NAAC | Academic institutional context | Academic environment, teaching/research systems, infrastructure, governance and student support | A++/A+ grades belong to the documented scheme/cycle. Parent-university or nursing-college grade is not an MD/MS department grade. |
| NQAS | Public-hospital quality signal | Quality and patient-safety systems in public facilities | Keep Certified vs Conditionality and expiry separate. PHC/SC certificates do not transfer to a medical-college teaching hospital. |
| NABH Eye Care Organisation | Relevant specialty-facility accreditation | Eye-care service quality and safety systems | Does not verify residency cataract numbers, subspecialty autonomy, current training permission or outcomes. |
| NABH entry-level certification | Foundational quality certification | Foundational quality and patient-safety processes under an entry-level programme | Never label this simply full NABH accreditation or compare it as equivalent to HCO/SHCO. |
| NABH targeted programmes | Scope-specific supplementary evidence | Only the system/service covered by the particular programme | Nursing Excellence, digital-health levels and blood-bank/ethics accreditation are not full hospital or postgraduate-course accreditation. |
| AERB | Applicable statutory safety prerequisite | Radiological safety and regulatory compliance | A legal safety requirement, not an academic prestige score. Verify live licence, equipment and permitted operations. |
| ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001 | Supporting management certification | Quality management / environmental management / occupational health and safety respectively | Not equivalent to NABL medical-testing competence, NABH clinical assessment or course recognition. ISO itself does not certify hospitals. |
| NIRF Medical / NIRF University | Ranking, not accreditation | Reported indicators such as teaching resources, research, graduation outcomes, inclusion and perception | A university ranking is not its medical-college rank. Not admission permission, hospital accreditation or evidence that every department is strong. |
| CGHS / ECHS / PM-JAY empanelment; awards | Not clinical or training accreditation | Payment-network eligibility or the award organiser's separate criteria | Empanelment, press awards, memberships and an application in progress must not be counted as full accreditation. |
| WFME recognition / WDOMS listing | Regulator recognition / directory context | Recognition of the accrediting agency’s process, or existence of a school in a directory | WFME recognition of NMC does not give each Indian college a separate WFME quality grade. WDOMS listing is not accreditation or an endorsement of PG training. |

## Rules for using this comparison

- This is a dated evidence register, not an exhaustive certification audit or a ranking of residency programmes. A missing match means not established in this research, never not accredited.
- The universe comes from the existing supplied 2025/2026 seat matrices, 2024/2025 allotments and earlier institution profiles. It is not a newly downloaded complete list of all state-counselling colleges. Original source records and unresolved duplicate identities remain visible.
- DNB is a qualification/counselling route, not a private ownership category. The Central / All India matrix also does not establish central-government ownership. Ownership is shown only where a previous researched profile supplies it.
- Current directory listing, certificate date range and admission-session permission are different facts. A date within range does not rule out suspension, withdrawal or a later scope change. An expired document does not prove that renewal was refused.
- A university/parent HEI NAAC grade is academic context; it is not automatically a separate grade for its medical college, hospital, every constituent campus or any MD/MS department. Affiliated colleges do not inherit the affiliating university grade.
- NBEMS records cover the exact named specialty and qualification. DrNB/FNB and diploma entries are labelled separately; their presence at a campus does not approve a DNB/MD/MS course there. Accredited seats are not counselling vacancies.
- NABH entry-level certification, Nursing Excellence, digital health and insurer empanelment must not be treated as full hospital accreditation. NABH laboratory certification is not NABL ISO 15189 accreditation.
- NABL and CAP cover the named laboratory and scope. An external reference laboratory or a lab elsewhere in the same chain cannot be assigned to the training campus. NABH hospital scope listing laboratory services does not itself establish NABL accreditation.
- Accreditation does not establish resident operating rights, case allocation, examination outcomes, non-toxic supervision, hostel safety, maternity support or timely stipend. Ask current residents and inspect department records.

## College questions

- First verify exact campus, qualification, specialty, permitted/recognised intake and admission year with NMC/NBEMS and the applicable counselling authority. For INIs use the relevant INI prospectus and statutory authority, rather than assuming an NMC database omission means an unrecognised course.
- Ask for the certificate number, awarding body, named legal entity, campus address, programme/standard, issue/expiry dates and complete scope annexure; check live suspension/withdrawal notices and written renewal evidence.
- For Pathology/Biochemistry, inspect NABL ISO 15189 scope for histopathology, cytopathology, haematology, molecular diagnostics and clinical biochemistry as relevant. CAP is an additional laboratory-quality signal; proficiency-testing participation alone is not CAP accreditation.
- For Ophthalmology, confirm the relevant Eye Care Organisation/hospital accreditation and the actual surgical site, then ask for primary-surgeon cataract numbers, subspecialty rotations and how cases are divided with fellows.
- For ENT/General Surgery, inspect OT safety, anaesthesia/ICU support, infection surveillance and clinical scope, then verify resident-led emergency and elective operating exposure independently.
- For Radiation Oncology/Nuclear Medicine/Radiodiagnosis, verify applicable AERB permissions, equipment/site scope and QA, in addition to course approval. AERB is a statutory safety gate, not a prestige award or a guarantee of hands-on training.
- For a female resident relocating from Hyderabad, separately verify safe accommodation, night transport, functioning Internal Committee, confidential grievance access, duty/rest rosters and maternity/medical leave with current women residents.
- Use optional quality credentials as a tie-breaker after teaching, resident case mix, supervision, cost, language, bonds and safety. Do not discard a strong government department solely because it lacks a matched voluntary private-hospital credential.

## Files

- `institution-accreditations.xlsx`: all-institution register, matched evidence, NBEMS/NMC programmes, pending campus identities, credential guide, state coverage, source register and written-check tracker.
- `institution-accreditations.csv`: one row per matched campus/evidence binding. This includes separate claims/context and empanelment labels; do not count all rows as full accreditations.
- `accreditation-pending-matches.csv`: possible identity matches only. These are NOT confirmed accreditations.
- `accreditation-institution-register.csv`: every input record, including records with no confirmed match.
- `institution-accreditations.json` contains institution/binding/source data and a `record_parts` array of relative JSON filenames. Concatenate those part arrays to reconstruct the complete `records` collection. The separate JS parts provide the same records offline without API calls.
