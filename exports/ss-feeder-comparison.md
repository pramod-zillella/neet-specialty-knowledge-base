# INI-SS versus NEET-SS: feeder comparison

Checked: 2026-10-18

Radiation Oncology/Radiotherapy and Pathology, MD and DNB; no generalisation to other parent specialties.

INI-SS is the superspecialty entrance examination. INI-CET is a different examination; there is no combined INI-CET-SS route in this comparison.

The compared documents belong to different admission cycles: NEET-SS 2025 (counselling in 2026) and INI-SS January 2027. This is a rule comparison, not a same-year seat comparison. Future rules may change before a current MD/DNB entrant finishes training.

## Differences that matter

- Radiation Oncology/Radiotherapy to Medical Oncology: permitted by NEET-SS 2025. In INI-SS January 2027, JIPMER explicitly lists MD/DNB Radiotherapy; PGIMER lists MD Radiotherapy or equivalent. AIIMS lists Medicine/Paediatrics, excluding this parent specialty under its listed rule.
- Pathology to Clinical Haematology: permitted by NEET-SS 2025, but the checked INI-SS AIIMS/PGIMER lists name Medicine/Paediatrics or Medicine; Pathology is not listed. Clinical Haematology and Haematopathology are different courses.
- Medical Genetics: NEET-SS 2025 permits any MD/MS/DNB broad specialty. The checked INI-SS lists are narrower: AIIMS Paediatrics/Medicine/Obstetrics & Gynaecology, PGIMER Paediatrics. Neither Pathology nor Radiation Oncology is listed.
- Pathology retains INI-specific options: Haematopathology, PGIMER Histopathology and NIMHANS Neuropathology, alongside AIIMS Oncopathology. AIIMS also lists MD Pathology for Tropical Infectious Diseases & Infection Control; DNB acceptance is not explicit there.
- A course name containing Pathology does not make MD/DNB Pathology a feeder: INI-SS Forensic Pathology lists Forensic Medicine/Forensic Medicine & Toxicology.

## Status legend

- **Yes:** Parent qualification explicitly listed, subject to all other rules and the actual course/seat offer.
- **No:** This parent specialty is not among the listed feeders for this institute/course; it is not a claim that the parent branch has no SS options.
- **Confirm equivalent degree:** MD in the parent specialty is listed with equivalent-degree wording; this table does not automatically settle DNB equivalence. Obtain current institute confirmation.
- **Confirm DNB acceptance:** The quoted feeder names MD only; DNB is not explicitly named for this course in the checked rule.
- **Not listed in NEET-SS 2025:** This named destination was not found in the checked NEET-SS 2025 feeder tables; it may be an INI-specific route, not an unrecognised course.

## System comparison

### What is being compared

**NEET-SS:** NEET-SS 2025 feeder permissions for DM/DrNB; exact course award and counselling matter.

**INI-SS:** INI-SS January 2027 DM/MCh eligibility, varying by institute/course. INI-CET is separate.

### Qualification is not inferred from the exam name

**NEET-SS:** Annexures A/B specify feeders. The Medical Genetics any-MD/MS/DNB rule does not open every Medical Group course.

**INI-SS:** Use the institute-specific feeder row, Part B and notices. A feeder at JIPMER may be excluded at AIIMS.

### MD versus DNB

**NEET-SS:** The positive routes in this focused comparison explicitly accept MD/DNB; preserve the exact listed parent specialty.

**INI-SS:** Some rows explicitly name MD/DNB; others name MD or equivalent, or MD only. Keep unresolved equivalence conditional.

### Question paper / assessment

**NEET-SS:** Relevant question-paper group matters: Medical Oncology Group, Medical Group for Genetics/Clinical Haematology, Pathology Group for Onco-Pathology.

**INI-SS:** Course-specific application/eligibility. January 2027 Part A describes Stage I for all and Stage II departmental assessment for AIIMS/PGIMER only; apply the full merit rules.

### Age and completion cut-offs

**NEET-SS:** Do not import INI age limits into NEET-SS; check the intended bulletin, qualification-completion date and counselling rules.

**INI-SS:** January 2027 Part A: base upper ages AIIMS 35, PGIMER 45, NIMHANS 37, SCTIMST 40 as of 1 January 2027; JIPMER no upper age limit. Relaxation/sponsorship exceptions and the NIMHANS all-category rule need individual checking. Qualification/tenure deadline stated as 31 January 2027.

### Seat and category

**NEET-SS:** A permitted feeder does not guarantee a course/college seat, category eligibility, rank or admission.

**INI-SS:** Part A and Part B seats may change. General, sponsored and foreign-national provisions are distinct; a qualifying course with zero applicable seats is not an available choice.

### No career guarantee

**NEET-SS:** Passing or obtaining a further qualification does not establish a pay premium or suitable employment.

**INI-SS:** An institute-specific SS route is an option, not a requirement for every parent-specialty job or a guaranteed career upgrade.
