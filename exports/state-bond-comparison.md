# MD/MS state and institution bond comparison

Checked 2026-10-18.

Your route: ordinary non-in-service MD/MS through MCC 50% All India Quota in state government colleges, across 18 preferred states/UTs. Central, deemed, DNB, AFMS and INI routes have separate contracts.

**MCC allotment does not waive an applicable service bond. Read the AIQ-specific years and evidence column first. In Andhra Pradesh, the checked one-year undertaking explicitly names State/CQ seats, so an AIQ duration remains unconfirmed. Delhi has no single duration: MAMC is one year from 2025–26 onwards; other campuses require their own rule.**

Do not assume Delhi is free of post-PG service bonds. MAMC circular dated 7 August 2025 expressly introduces one year / Rs20 lakh for PG entrants from 2025–26 onwards, AIQ and state quota. DU's 2026 Rs10 lakh surety is a separate course-exit bond. Other Delhi campuses need their own current authority/cohort check.

A mixed-date evidence comparison, not a fully verified 2026 national bond schedule or a legal enforceability opinion. Unresolved terms remain unknown, never zero. Sources checked 18 October 2026; apply subsequent amendments.

## Read the obligations separately

- Service bond: work owed after successful completion. The quoted amount is a default liability, not an annual fee or a charge automatically payable by every graduate.
- Exit bond: liability for non-joining, resignation or discontinuation at specified stages. This is separate from compulsory service and counselling security-deposit forfeiture.
- A bank guarantee or surety secures an obligation. Do not add it again to the underlying bond amount. Do not automatically add service and exit penalties; their triggers and interaction need the executed contract.
- AIQ/MCC admission does not itself exempt a candidate from the admitting institution's applicable state service bond. Central/INI, private/deemed, state government and DNB contracts must be checked separately.
- This comparison concerns ordinary non-in-service MD/MS entrants unless a row says otherwise. Diploma, DNB/DrNB, direct six-year courses, sponsored and in-service obligations are not interchangeable.
- An old form, missing upload or silent prospectus is not proof of no bond. A checked 2026 rule is an admission-cycle snapshot, not a guarantee of the rule when a future graduate finishes.

## Written college confirmation checklist

- Obtain the latest government order, admission-year prospectus, corrigenda and the exact unsigned college bond before choice locking.
- Confirm campus, qualification, branch, quota, admission year and whether any prior MBBS bond remains. Ask for the applicable clause in writing.
- Confirm service duration, who posts you, location, specialty use, remuneration and whether a posting is guaranteed. A service obligation is not a guaranteed permanent government job.
- Confirm maternity/medical leave, safety, housing, transfer and extension rules. Do not assume the service will be in Hyderabad or near home.
- Confirm the deadline for offering/accepting a posting, release if no posting is made, and the process for obtaining a written discharge/NOC.
- Confirm whether NEET-SS/INI-SS admission permits deferment, the bank guarantee required, and whether fellowship/observership has different treatment.
- Confirm the financial formula: principal, pro-rata reduction, stipend/salary refund, interest, study expenditure and recovery costs. Ask how overlapping obligations interact.
- Check exit dates, non-joining rules, remaining-course tuition, counselling deposit forfeiture and debarment separately.
- Ask which originals, guarantees and sureties are requested and when they are released. A document describing retention is not an independent assessment of its legal enforceability.
- Retain the signed contract and written answers. Recheck amendments before joining; seek qualified advice on an actual disputed demand.
